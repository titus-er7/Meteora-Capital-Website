Vik Mittal, CFA

Managing Member & CIO

Vik Mittal is the Founder, Managing Member and Chief Investment Officer of Meteora Capital, LLC.

Prior to founding Meteora Capital, Vik was an investment professional and Partner at Glazer Capital Management from June 2005 through December 2021. As one of only two Portfolio Managers during his tenure, Vik was responsible for identifying, evaluating and executing investments across a broad range of event-driven and special situations strategies. His investment responsibilities encompassed structured credit and equity, merger arbitrage, convertible securities, high-yield debt and special purpose acquisition companies (SPACs), with a focus on opportunities across the capital structure.

Prior to joining Glazer Capital, Vik worked in the Technology, Media and Telecommunications (TMT) Investment Banking Group at Raymond James & Associates from August 2002 to May 2005. Based in Palo Alto, California, he served as an Analyst and Junior Associate, focusing on middle-market mergers and acquisitions, private placements and capital-raising transactions.

Vik received his Master of Business Administration (MBA) from New York University's Leonard N. Stern School of Business in 2012. He previously attended the University of Florida, completing his studies in 2002.

Kevin S. Gahwyler, CFA

Managing Director

Kevin S. Gahwyler, CFA, is Managing Director, Co-Chief Operating Officer and Director of Business Development at Meteora Capital, LLC.

Prior to joining Meteora Capital, Kevin served as Chief Operating Officer and Director of Business Development at Measure 8 Venture Partners from March 2019 to September 2021. Prior to joining Measure 8, Kevin was President and Chief Financial Officer of Navy Capital from May 2018 to January 2019, where he focused on investment management and business operations.

Prior to joining Navy Capital, Kevin served as Chief Operating Officer and Director of Business Development at Twin Capital Management from February 2010 to April 2018. During his tenure, he oversaw business operations, investor relations and capital raising for the firm's event-driven investment strategies, including merger arbitrage and special situations.

Earlier in his career, Kevin held senior positions at KenCole Capital, CDG Holdings, Pequot Capital and Sagamore Hill, with responsibilities spanning portfolio management, business development, investor relations and capital raising.

Kevin received his Master of International Management in International Finance from Thunderbird School of Global Management in 1993 and his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Fairfield University in 1985. He holds the Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation.

Joe Levy, CPA

Chief Operating & Financial Officer, Chief Compliance Officer

Joe Levy is the Chief Operating and Financial Officer of Meteora Capital, LLC.

Prior to joining Meteora Capital, Joe served as Chief Financial Officer at Tegean Capital Management from November 2015 to October 2021 and subsequently as Chief Compliance Officer through August 2022. Previously, Joe was a Financial Planning and Analysis Manager at Millennium Management from April 2012 to November 2015.

Prior to joining Millennium Management, Joe served as a Senior Analyst at Goldman Sachs from January 2010 to April 2012. Earlier in his career, he worked as a Senior Associate at KPMG from August 2007 to January 2010.

Joe received his Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Finance from New York University's Leonard N. Stern School of Business in 2015 and his Bachelor of Science in Business Administration in Accounting from the University of Pittsburgh in 2007.

Meteora Capital, LLC

Event-driven investing with a long-term lens

Meteora was formed in 2021 as an investment adviser specializing in event-driven equity and credit strategies. We generate compelling risk-adjusted returns for its institutional clientele by employing a disciplined, systematic approach to event-driven investments, while aligning our interests with clients by investing significant internal capital alongside them. We also provide customized structured equity and credit investments to companies interested in solutions tailored to their financing needs.

Our mission

Our mission is to build a best-in-class investment firm with a focus on event-driven investments. We intend to leverage our distinctive platform in our public markets hedge fund, which seeks to optimize performance while minimizing drawdowns. Within our long-duration private markets funds, we aspire to be a true end-to-end partner in the SPAC ecosystem, capitalizing on a combination of both public and private investment opportunities.

What’s in a name?

Meteora [Me•te•o•ra]

Proper Noun

We are often asked where the name “Meteora” comes from. Literally translated as “middle of the sky,” “suspended in the air,” or “in the heavens above,” Meteora is a place on the plain of Thessaly in northern Greece where one of the largest and most precipitously built complexes of Eastern Orthodox monasteries in Greece stands.
